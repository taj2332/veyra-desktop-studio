## 15-second demonstration

1. Show an animated scene and desktop clock.
2. Switch Studio → Focus → Night.
3. Start a focus session and show the desktop countdown.
4. Remix a signature or match a photo palette.
5. Show Export pack and the free/open-source message.

Describe this as a public preview. Local testing reached about 60 FPS at 1080p on a GTX 1650; avoid promises of 60 FPS on every device, native-4K photo detail, video playback, arbitrary taskbar skins or a hosted gallery.
