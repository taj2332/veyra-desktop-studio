# Create your first Veyra pack

1. Explore the starter collection and select a scene.
2. Change the accent, clock, animation, motion and light/dark preference.
3. Use **Save a copy**, or open **Creator studio → Create from this scene**.
4. Enter a pack name, your creator name, description and license.
5. Export the `.veyra.json` file.
6. Share the file and a preview wherever you publish your work. The recipient uses **Import pack** or drops it into Veyra.

For your own photo, use **Create a pack → Use my photo**. PNG and JPEG are supported. Files must be at most 16 MB, 8192 pixels on each side and 40 megapixels. The photo is embedded in the exported pack. Photo packs support a gentle pan, atmosphere effects and an optional live desktop clock.

Importing only adds a pack to the library; it never changes Windows. Customize a pack and press **Apply to desktop** separately.

## Rights and credit

Share only artwork you created or have permission to redistribute. If the pack is only for yourself, keep the default personal-use statement. CC0, CC BY 4.0 and similar licenses have different obligations; choose deliberately. Do not relabel someone else's content as your own.

The starter procedural artwork is CC0. The two generated backgrounds have separate provenance and redistribution notes in ARTWORK.md. Veyra does not verify the rights statement in imported packs.

## What travels in a pack

- Creator name, description and license.
- Scene choice or embedded image.
- Accent color, light/dark preference, animation, clock style / placement, motion and native Windows taskbar preferences.

No computer username, original image path, installed app list, scripts or Windows backup is exported.

## Flow recipes

Open Flow studio to choose Studio, Focus or Night. Remix a procedural signature or match the palette to a photo, then save a copy. Mood and scene seed travel in the pack; active focus deadlines and computer preferences stay private.
